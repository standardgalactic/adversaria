## Correction recorded during drafting

The statement that an odd signed cycle "is an H^1 class" is exact only for the Z/2-valued identification model. For general sheaves H^1 may witness an obstruction, but vanishing H^1 is not a universal guarantee of global sections, and pairwise-compatible local sections can fail to glue for structures not reducible to signed binary identifications. The manuscript therefore (1) states the general gluing problem, (2) proves the signed case as a theorem, (3) gives H^1 as the interpretation of that case only, (4) states broader Cech machinery as an extension without claiming universality.
